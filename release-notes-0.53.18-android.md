# Prime Minutes Android 0.53.18 — Release Notes

- Easier to use with a screen reader: buttons and rows across the app now announce what they do, and touch targets meet the 48dp minimum.
- Meeting timestamps now read h:mm:ss once a recording passes the first hour.
- Refreshed look: a calmer Sky color palette with consistent spacing, corner rounding, and text styles.
- Empty screens explain themselves: the transcript says whether the audio was silent or just not transcribed yet, and podcast search and feeds say why a list is empty.
- Podcast series and YouTube results open from clearly labelled cards.
- A status bar stays pinned at the top of the meeting screen while transcription or summarization runs, so progress is always visible.
- Safer during a run: transcripts are read-only, speaker naming is locked, naming can be undone, and clearing Ask history asks first.
- Ask is more useful: answers can be selected and copied, the library Ask starts new conversations, and transcript search jumps the player to each match.
