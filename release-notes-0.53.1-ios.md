# Prime Minutes iOS 0.53.1 — Release Notes

- Easier to use with VoiceOver: controls announce what they do, and key targets meet the 44pt minimum.
- Empty screens use native empty states: library, transcript, People directory, and podcast search all explain what is missing.
- Refreshed look: shared color, spacing, and text tokens across screens.
- A status bar stays pinned at the top of the meeting screen while transcription or summarization runs, so progress is always visible.
- Safer during a run: renaming is gated, editing sheets refuse empty drafts, unsafe menu actions are disabled, and engine settings lock.
- Small confirmations: copying the transcript shows a message, deleting a person gives haptic feedback, and a hint explains what is still pending.
- Clearer import errors: download failures name the cause, including files over the 500 MB limit.
- Under the hood: the meeting screen was restructured into smaller files with no visible changes, keeping builds fast.
