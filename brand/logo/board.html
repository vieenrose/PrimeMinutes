What we heard

The brief

A private on-device meeting-minutes app (iOS + Android) for a zh-TW audience. Minutes here means the record, not the duration: the mark must say 'the transcript, distilled to the key line' — calm, precise, trustworthy, the opposite of chatty AI assistants.

précisprivéposéépuré

Success: reads at 16 px as an app icon, works in one colour, distinct from chat-bubble/waveform/brain marks, no time-clichés.

Concept A

Key line

A transcript card whose key line escapes the edge, pinned by a dot.

  • Reads as 'the record, distilled' in one glance
  • Strong at 16 px: tile + protruding line survives the app-store grid
  • No time-clichés, no chat bubbles, no gradient
